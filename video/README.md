# SoC2611 - explainer videos

Short narrated videos. Each one describes the state of the day it was made.

| file | made | content |
|---|---|---|
| `SoC2611_status.mp4` | 10 Nov 2026 | status: how the routing closed (641 to 0), DRC and density results, the clock timing finding and its fix, the dates |
| `SoC2611_explained.mp4` | 10 Nov 2026 | the whole chip: blocks, data flow, memory, pins, numbers, boot |
| `SoC2611_pinout.mp4` | 9 Nov 2026 | the pads, their sides and functions |
| `SoC2611_shorts_explained.mp4` | 9 Nov 2026 | what the routing shorts inside and around the SRAM macros were and where they came from |
| `SoC2611_rounds_and_ask.mp4` | 9 Nov 2026 | the repair rounds of 8-9 November and the open question about the delivery date |

The scripts that render them are in `SoC2611-main/Flow/scripts/signoff/video/`.
