# SoC2611 - explainer videos

Short narrated videos made during the routing closure (9 November 2026). They describe the state of that day.

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `SoC2611_pinout.mp4` | the 48 pads, their sides and functions |
| `SoC2611_shorts_explained.mp4` | what the routing shorts inside and around the SRAM macros were and where they came from |
| `SoC2611_rounds_and_ask.mp4` | the repair rounds of 8-9 November and the open question about the delivery date |

The scripts that render them are in `SoC2611-main/Flow/scripts/signoff/video/`.
