SoC2611 power estimate (static analysis, 13 November 2026)

Method
  OpenSTA in OpenROAD dcf36133, report_power on the routed database with the parasitics of the routed wires, one run
  per corner (Liberty of the standard cells, IO cells and SRAM macros of IHP-Open-PDK dcb03b1e, same corner).
  No simulation vectors: every input pin and every register output toggles with a rate of 0.1 per clock cycle, and
  the tool carries that rate through the logic. Clocks run at the frequency of the constraints: input clock 10 ns
  (100 MHz), system clock 20 ns (50 MHz). The routed layout is the one of 13 November; release v.3.0.0 later added
  34 buffers and 26 antenna diodes to it (about 0.01 % of the cells), and v.3.0.2 is v.3.0.0 without two row fillers.
  This is an estimate. No silicon has been measured.

Result at a 100 MHz input clock (50 MHz system clock), toggle rate 0.1, in W

  group            slow 1.08 V 125 C   typical 1.20 V 25 C   fast 1.32 V -40 C
  registers            0.1757              0.2186                0.2677
  logic                0.0623              0.0791                0.0998
  clock tree           0.0724              0.0926                0.1186
  SRAM macros          0.1902              0.2292                0.2912
  pad cells            0.0034              0.0042                0.0053
  total                0.5038              0.6241                0.7827
  of it leakage        0.0006              0.0001                0.0003

  Registers, clock tree and SRAM macros take about 87 % of the total. Their power depends on the clock, not much on
  what the software does, so the estimate is a fair first figure for any program.

Model
  P = P_leakage + P_dynamic x (f_system / 50 MHz)
  with P_dynamic = total minus leakage from the table, at the same toggle rate. Leakage is below 1 mW at every corner.
  At the rated system clock of each corner (static timing on the routed wires: slow 29.4 MHz, typical 45.5 MHz,
  fast 64.4 MHz) this gives about 0.30 W slow, 0.57 W typical and 1.01 W fast.
  A program with more activity than 0.1 per cycle raises mainly the logic share (about 13 %).

Supply current at the analysed point (fast corner, 0.78 W at 1.32 V): about 0.59 A on VDD, carried by 4 VDD and
4 VSS pads. Static voltage drop at that point: release/v.3.0.2/checks/supply_drop.txt (VDD 71 mV, VSS 122 mV worst).
