# SoC2611 - status 7 November 2026 (routing closure in progress)

The preliminary release v.1.0.0 (GDS, CDL, Verilog) is unchanged. This update adds the physical-design work since the
audit (issue IHP-GmbH/Open-Silicon-MPW#79): flow scripts, floorplan files, the routing-closure log and the two
problem briefs, so that the final release can be reproduced and reviewed.

## What was done
- The routing shorts of the preliminary release came from the floorplan (a column of 28 SRAMs 26 um from the pad
  ring). The chip was re-floorplanned by hand: 298 SRAM macros grouped by the logic that drives them, pin rows facing
  standard-cell strips, 180 um routing highways, every macro >= 100 um from the pad ring.
- A measured campaign (DPASS: placement + clock tree + global route per candidate, scored on global-route overflow
  and then on the detailed-route violation map) over 20 floorplan variants; see
  `SoC2611-main/Flow/ROUTING_CLOSURE_LOG.md` (dated log) and `SoC2611-main/Flow/floorplan/` (placements, blockages,
  pictures, problem briefs).
- Best routed state so far: floorplan "H10" (H4 macro placement + two placement blockages), detailed route at pass 40
  with 1,290 violations, down from 392,856 at pass 0. About 1,200 of the remaining violations are Metal4 shorts
  between signal wires and the SRAM macros' power-pin stripes inside the macros (the RM_IHPSG13 LEF leaves Metal4
  open between its power stripes; the global router overflows pass-through nets across the macros). Root cause and
  evidence: `SoC2611-main/Flow/floorplan/ROUTING_PROBLEM_H10.md`.
- Fix in progress: floorplan "H12" opens a north-south passage through the CPU block (the two 1024x32 caches moved to
  the east margin, the two 64x22 tag macros moved west), which removes the pass-through demand across the biggest
  macros; its global route is being checked for guides inside macros before detailed routing starts.

## Open for the final release
Routing closure (zero shorts/spacing), antenna repair, density fill and the PDK density deck, the IHP minimum DRC
with retained result databases (filler tables on overlapping clips + full-chip where it completes), LVS of the
released GDS against the released CDL (SRAM/IO as library cells), waiver records, hold/setup timing report,
testbenches and simulation logs, a self-contained synthesis script, then a tagged commit with GDS/CDL SHA-256.
