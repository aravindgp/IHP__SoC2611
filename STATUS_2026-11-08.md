# SoC2611 - status 8 November 2026 (routing closure in progress)

The preliminary release v.1.0.0 (GDS, CDL, Verilog) is unchanged. This update adds the physical-design work of
7/8 November: the root cause of the remaining routing shorts, the floorplan variants H13/H14, the ECO method now
used to close the route, the rehearsal of the post-route chain, and all scripts, so the final release can be
reproduced and reviewed. Dated log: `SoC2611-main/Flow/ROUTING_CLOSURE_LOG.md`.

## Root cause of the residual shorts (all runs)
- The detailed-route residue of every floorplan (H10 ~1,100, H12 ~900 violations) is Metal4 shorts between signal
  wires and the SRAM macros' power-pin stripes inside the macros. The signal wires are pass-through nets: the
  global router (`-allow_congestion`) overflows them across the macros because no legal vertical crossing of a
  RM_IHPSG13 macro exists (Metal1-4 blocked inside the macro, no metal above TopMetal1 in SG13CMOS5L; TopMetal1 is
  the horizontal layer).
- The pass-through demand comes from standard cells placed in the dead spaces of the macro placement: the 60-130 um
  pockets between two macro rows whose pin edges face each other, the 100 um strips between the pad ring and the
  edge-row macros, and the 20 um blank gaps between stacked rows. 98 of the 112 crossing nets of H12 had a pin in
  such a space (`Flow/floorplan/H12_pass3_*.png`, `ROUTING_CLOSURE_LOG.md` 8 Nov 01:35-02:35 UTC).
- Levers measured and rejected: routing obstructions inside the macros before the global route (the result is
  byte-identical, the capacity there is already zero), more congestion iterations, incremental global re-route of
  the crossing nets, moving the crossing cells before a fresh global route (H15: a fresh route pushes other nets
  through the same rows).

## Closure method now running: side-move ECO rounds
On a routed snapshot, every net that still crosses a macro gets its minority-side cells moved across the macro row
(blockage/macro/channel aware targets, legalizer capped at 40 um so no cell crosses back), the touched nets are
re-routed by an incremental global route and a plain detailed route with everything else frozen
(`pins_side.py`, `eco_sidemove.tcl`, `eco_round.sh`). Measured: inside-macro shorts 687 -> 439 in one round on the
H13 pass-22 snapshot (camera FIFO 238 -> 61); each round needs ~20 re-route passes to bring the total back below
its starting point. Rounds are chained, each re-planned from the previous round's DRC report.

## Post-route chain rehearsed on the H13 pass-27 snapshot
- Bondpad pins + chip DEF/ODB/Verilog: 3 min. DEF -> GDS (KLayout): 60 s, 750 MB. The 16 "empty cell" messages of
  the streamer are the RM_IHPSG13 BITKIT placeholder sub-cells, empty in the PDK's own SRAM GDS and in release
  v.1.0.0 alike (`gds_cell_check.py`); the bit-cell arrays are complete.
- Antenna: 6,052 violating nets before repair; `repair_antennas` (sg13cmos5l_antennanp) inserted 10,927 diodes and
  left 581 nets after one iteration; the re-route of the touched nets is the cost (`eco_antenna.tcl`).
