The same counts as v.3.0.0: the two row fillers that v.3.0.2 leaves out have no devices and no nets, and the LVS
view leaves row fillers out of the netlist and dissolves them into the top cell of the layout.

Scope, the changes that make the run finish on this chip, and the negative control (one via removed and one bridge
added, reported as exactly those three mismatched nets) are those described in the LVS summary of v.3.0.0: the same
view recipe and the same deck were used.
  - Every standard cell with its devices and every connection of the chip top.
  - SRAM macros and pad cells are black boxes with their pins; inside them only drawn metal and vias are kept.
  - The layout is the one before fill. The seal ring instance is left out.

What LVS cannot see: a pin without a net in the netlist has no net in the layout either. The check of every input
pin for a net (open_inputs.txt) and the simulation of the routed netlist (simulation_summary.txt) cover it.
