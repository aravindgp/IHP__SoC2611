Voltage drop of the core supply on the layout of this release (14 November 2026)

Method
  OpenROAD PDNSim on the routed database with the wire file of the routed nets. Fast corner, 1.32 V, total power
  0.78 W (100 MHz input clock, one signal in ten switching per cycle). The two supply rails of the pad ring are in
  the model as wires, and the sources are the four supply pads of each net, one per chip side. An analysis that puts
  ideal sources on the power straps of the core does not see the rails of the pad ring at all: it shows 17 mV and
  27 mV for the layout before the added metal.

Result                          VDD worst   VDD average   VSS worst   VSS average
  before the added metal          217 mV       150 mV       205 mV       149 mV
  this release                     71 mV        48 mV       122 mV        89 mV
  "Before" was measured on the repaired route of this release without the added metal. The supply metal of every
  earlier file (v.2.4.0, candidate v.2.5.0 and before) is the same as in that layout.

What was weak
  Each chip side has one core supply pad per net. Only the west and east pads were tied to the power grid, over 125
  and 126 TopMetal1 straps that start at the pad ring; the current of a pad reached those straps along one Metal4
  rail of the pad ring, 15.8 um wide. The north and south pads were not tied to the core at all.

What was added (0.762 mm2 of TopMetal1 in 256 boxes, 516 vias, all in the empty strip between pad ring and core)
  - VDD, west and east: one TopMetal1 bar per side over the VDD rail of the pad ring, 24 um wide, 5.95 mm long.
  - VSS, west and east: a TopMetal1 bar per side over the VSS rail, in 125 pieces between the VDD straps, each piece
    joined to the Metal4 rail by vias (255 vias per side).
  - VDD, north and south: a TopMetal1 bar on the VDD side of the core ring and a tie from the supply pad to it.
  - VSS, north and south: three Metal4 ties per pad from the pad to the VSS side of the core ring.
  No signal wire, cell or pad moved.

Checks of the added metal
  - IHP minimum rule set on the whole chip, tables topmetal1, topvia1, metalslits, pad, passiv: 0 violations;
    Metal2 to Metal4 rules in windows around the four VSS pads: clean. The complete rule set on the GDS of this
    release is in drc_minimum_fullfill.txt.
  - Power grid check of the router: VDD and VSS pass.
  - Short check in the GDS: starting from a VDD bar, the connected metal reaches the other three VDD bars and the
    VDD rail, and none of the six VSS probe points (bars, rail, ring, ties).
  - LVS matches with the bars and ties in the layout (lvs_summary.txt).
  - Density with the bars: TopMetal1 34.2 % (density.txt).
