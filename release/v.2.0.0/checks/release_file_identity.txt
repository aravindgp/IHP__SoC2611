Identity of the released full-fill GDS and the GDS the checks ran on (12 November 2026)

The minimum rule set and the density check ran on the full-fill GDS as it left the fill and corner clean-up steps.
The release packaging then renames the top cell to SoC2611 and saves the file with the KLayout options of the
Open-Silicon-MPW flow. To show that nothing else changed, the two parts gds/SoC2611_fullfill.gds.gz.00.part and
.01.part were taken from the pushed branch, joined with cat and compared with the checked file cell by cell
(KLayout LayoutDiff, every cell paired with its counterpart, all shapes and all instances).
